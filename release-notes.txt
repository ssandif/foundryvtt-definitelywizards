v2.0.0 15/11/2026
Compatibility
- Requires Foundry VTT v14 (compatibility minimum/verified 14)

What's New (Players & GMs)
- Redesigned character and item sheets with resizable windows, rich-text editors, custom actor/item/macro artwork, Wizard/Wild-themed stat cards with Lockout/Arrest badges, and friendly linked default tokens
- Added starting Prop hints and one-click Add to Props on Attributes, Costuming (+1 Wizard / -1 Wild) buttons on Props and chat cards, and automatic 10 starting Wizard Spells (+ Add Starting Spells), direct 1d6/Wit cast buttons, and improvised Wild Spell casting
- Enforced Wizard/Wild stat-6 lockouts with automatic opposite-stat reset to 2 on success, plus arrest alerts at Wild > 6 or when the whole party reaches Wild = 6 simultaneously
- Upgraded chat cards with Wizard/Wild styling, SUCCESS/FAIL outcomes, Cover (+1 Wild), Undo Cover, Couldn't Disguise (+1 Wild), Consequences rolling, Wit Bonus (2d6kl) dice breakdowns, and post-action stat transition ordering
- Updated exam RollTables and added MonsterFactoryCreature/MonsterFactoryTrait tables plus an interactive Practical Exam generator (GM journal entry or whispered chat)
- Added GM Helpers and Player Macros compendiums, drag-to-hotbar support for stat rolls/Spells/Props/Items, Shift-click Wit Bonus casting, and automatic slot 1-4 player hotbar seeding (autoPopulateHotbar world setting)

Under the Hood (Code & Tooling)
- Migrated sheets from legacy AppV1 to ApplicationV2 (ActorSheetV2 & ItemSheetV2) with ProseMirror editors and Foundry hot-reload/setup metadata
- Replaced template.json with TypeDataModel schemas (CharacterDataModel & DefinitelyWizardsItemDataModel) and legacy field migration
- Replaced direct HTML chat mutations with event-sourced ChatMessage flags (resolvedMessageId) and modularized dice/macro helpers
- Replaced Less & Gulp with native CSS scoped to .definitelywizards sheets and chat cards
- Switched compendiums to tracked JSON sources (packs/_source/) with automated LevelDB builds (@foundryvtt/foundryvtt-cli & classic-level), Biome linting, TypeScript (checkJs) typechecking, Lefthook pre-commit hooks, and GitHub Actions CI/Release workflows
